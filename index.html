What are Life Processes?

Life processes are the basic activities performed by living organisms to maintain life.

Nutrition

Nutrition is the process by which organisms obtain and use food for energy, growth, repair and maintenance.

Respiration

Respiration is the process through which cells obtain usable energy from food.

Transportation

Transportation involves movement of materials within an organism.

Excretion

Excretion is the removal of metabolic waste products from the body.
